Dr. Yuan Yao has been named a recipient of the Young Investigator Meeting Award by the International Society of Exposure Science (ISES) as part of the 2026 ISES Annual Awards.

## Award Announcement

The International Society of Exposure Science announced its 2026 Annual Award winners, recognizing individuals who have made significant contributions to the field of exposure science.

**Announcement:** [2026 International Society of Exposure Science Annual Award Winners](https://www.intlexposurescience.org/2026-ises-annual-award-winners/)

**Published:** August 4, 2026

## Young Investigator Meeting Award

The Young Investigator Meeting Award encourages student and early-career researcher participation at ISES Annual Meetings. Dr. Yao was recognized as a recipient of the award while serving as a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Environmental Health Sciences at the UCLA Fielding School of Public Health.

## About the Recognition

The announcement highlights Dr. Yao's work at the intersection of air pollution, climate change, and human health, including research on wildfire smoke exposure, non-exhaust emissions from transportation sources, and community-engaged environmental health research.
