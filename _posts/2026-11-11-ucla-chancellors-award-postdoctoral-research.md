Dr. Yuan Yao has received the 2026 UCLA Chancellor’s Award for Postdoctoral Research, one of eight scholars across the university honored for their research excellence and scholarly achievements.

## Award Announcement

The UCLA Fielding School of Public Health highlighted Dr. Yao’s research on air pollution and public health, including her work on the aftermath of the 2025 Los Angeles County wildfires. Dr. Yao is a postdoctoral researcher in Dr. Yifang Zhu’s research group in the Department of Environmental Health Sciences.

**Announcement:** [UCLA Fielding School of Public Health researcher receives 2026 Chancellor’s Award for Postdoctoral Research](https://ph.ucla.edu/news-events/news/ucla-fielding-school-public-health-researcher-receives-2026-chancellors-award)

**Published:** November 11, 2026

**Award Ceremony:** November 10, 2026, at UCLA

## About the Recognition

The announcement highlights Dr. Yao’s leadership in studies showing that harmful pollutants can persist inside homes long after outdoor air quality improves following wildfires, helping inform community recovery efforts. It also recognizes her work identifying particulate emissions from electric vehicle fast-charging stations.

Other contributions featured in the announcement include installing a public air quality monitoring network in West Los Angeles and supporting the UCLA Aliso Canyon Disaster Health Research Study.
